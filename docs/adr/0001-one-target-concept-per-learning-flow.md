# Use one target concept per learning flow

Each Learning Flow pursues exactly one Target Concept and its Mastery Criterion, and every item of Learning Evidence is attributed to that Concept alone. Its Diagnostic may probe only prepared Supporting Concepts whose dependency and screening source passed Concept Preparation Gates, and may retain Flow-scoped Diagnostic Findings without changing their Concept Progress or creating Learning Evidence. Non-blocking findings may adapt pedagogy inside the Target Concept Flow. Before generating a prerequisite probe, the Flow checks accepted Concept Progress for every Required Supporting Concept. A Current Milestone of Independent or Durable establishes readiness directly only when the Supporting Concept identity and relevant Mastery Rubric, criterion, and source-basis versions align with the current Diagnostic Plan; any relevant version change, lower milestone, or absent progress receives the same brief screening used for knowledge learned elsewhere. Remaining Required Supporting Concepts are checked in the Plan's dependency order. Positive readiness for every one is required for the recommended route and direct post-Diagnostic Independent eligibility, but not for an explicit Direct Learning Choice into Target Learning and Practice. A Prerequisite Readiness Check first accepts the learner's self-report. “Not known” or “unsure” ends that check neutrally and may produce a recommendation without a test; “known” only routes to one small representative no-assistance probe and never establishes readiness on assertion alone. Confirmed performance is sufficient for this routing check; it does not require a redundant Independent Test, create Learning Evidence, establish a Mastery Milestone, or schedule review work for that Supporting Concept. A source gap cannot be converted into a learner readiness outcome.

A Conclusive prerequisite gap, a learner-declared unknown/unsure prerequisite, or terminal Unconfirmed prerequisite performance stops further Diagnostic probing at the first sufficient result and presents the appropriate neutral or conclusive Prerequisite Learning Recommendation. Lower-priority unprobed prerequisites and Target dimensions remain unknown. The learner may explicitly start a separate Learning Flow in which the Supporting Concept becomes the Target Concept, choose Target Learning and Practice directly, or leave; the current Flow neither teaches the Supporting Concept nor automatically creates or starts another Flow. Choosing the separate Flow suspends the original Target Flow. On learner-controlled return, aligned Independent or Durable progress satisfies that prerequisite directly, while lower/absent progress receives a brief readiness recheck; Diagnostic then continues from committed state without repeating confirmed areas. Choosing Direct Learning preserves all Findings and unknowns, creates no prerequisite readiness or Evidence, and makes only the normal post-Practice Independent path available. Technical Unavailable makes no readiness claim. This preserves single-Concept evidence and review ownership while leaving the final learning choice to the learner.

After prerequisites are ready, Diagnostic probes only the Target Readiness Set. Positive confirmation of that complete Set authorizes a fresh Independent Test but creates no Evidence and reveals no Diagnostic feedback before it. A Conclusive Target gap routes to Target Learning and Practice, where a learner-safe Diagnostic Summary may explain confirmed strengths, teaching priorities, and unknowns; confirmed strengths may compress or skip corresponding instruction and confirmed gaps may focus it. Unconfirmed Target performance consumes remaining Plan-authorized probes before routing neutrally at the termination limit. No unprobed Mastery Rubric criterion is treated as known.
