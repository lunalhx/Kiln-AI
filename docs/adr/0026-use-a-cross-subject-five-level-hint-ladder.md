# Use a cross-subject five-level Hint Ladder

Phase 0 will use one cross-subject Hint Ladder: H0 None, H1 Orient by restating the goal or exposed conditions, H2 Cue toward a relevant concept, source, or representation, H3 Strategy suggesting a method or sequence, H4 Scaffold revealing a next step or partial reasoning, and H5 Reveal providing the complete reasoning and answer. Every H1–H5 exposure is appended to the Task Attempt's Assistance Trace with content, source, Skill, model, version, and time metadata. Hint requests normally advance monotonically to the next unused level; an explicit request for the answer may jump to H5 but records the jump and cannot disguise intermediate disclosure. Before the first Hint on an Independent Test or Review, Kiln-AI warns the learner and converts the attempt to Practice upon consent. Practice submissions may be assessed as assisted performance but cannot create Independent Evidence. H5 closes the attempt as Solution Revealed without Assessment, after which the graph uses Teach-back or a fresh equivalent task. Procedural Clarification remains outside the Hint Ladder, and subject or Capability Skills may specialize Hint content but not redefine level semantics.
