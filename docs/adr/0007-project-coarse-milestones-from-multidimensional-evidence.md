# Project coarse milestones from multidimensional evidence

Concept Progress will expose only the coarse Mastery Milestones Unassessed, Learning, Independent, and Durable. “Understood” will be represented by evidence such as a Teach-back result, while “Assisted” will describe an attempt through its hint level and assistance trace; neither is a Concept-level state. Pedagogy Policy reads the milestone, current Learning Stage, and recent multidimensional evidence. Accepted Learning Evidence remains the source of truth, with Concept Progress maintained as a deterministic, rebuildable projection rather than a mutable mastery claim owned by an Agent.
