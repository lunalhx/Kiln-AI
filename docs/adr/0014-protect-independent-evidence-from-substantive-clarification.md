# Protect independent evidence from substantive clarification

During an open Task Attempt, the Clarification Gate will distinguish Procedural Clarification from Substantive Clarification before responding. A procedural response may only restate interface behavior, required format, notation, or conditions already exposed by the Task Package; it is recorded but does not disqualify an Independent Test or Review attempt. Any explanation of relevant knowledge, suggested method, revealed reasoning step, new example, or narrowing of the answer is substantive assistance and must be appended to the Assistance Trace. Before delivering substantive or ambiguously classified help during an independent attempt, Kiln-AI tells the learner that the attempt will no longer count as independent and asks whether to continue; consent converts the attempt purpose to Practice before routing the request to the Hint or Explain Teaching Node Profile, while refusal returns to the unchanged task. Independent eligibility cannot be restored later in that attempt, even after a correct answer, and must be earned on a fresh equivalent task. Clarification is therefore an interaction gate, not a sixth Teaching Node Profile.
