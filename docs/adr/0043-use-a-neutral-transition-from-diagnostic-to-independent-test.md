# Use a neutral transition from Diagnostic to Independent Test

After every submitted Diagnostic Attempt, Kiln-AI will show only a Neutral Transition whenever another Diagnostic task or a fresh Independent Test follows. It may update completed-versus-maximum progress but must not reveal correctness, the answer, missing rules, prerequisite findings, or other targeted feedback. Conclusive and Unconfirmed Diagnostic Findings create no evidence and follow the Plan-owned continuation and terminal routing rules rather than ending the stage after one Attempt. This prevents cross-attempt feedback from becoming unrecorded assistance before later no-assistance performance.

Once the route enters Target Learning and Practice, Kiln-AI may show a Diagnostic Summary containing only learner-safe confirmed strengths, teaching priorities, and unknown areas before using the same sanitized Findings for pedagogy. A Prerequisite Learning Recommendation may name the Supporting Concept and explain why it is recommended, but does not reveal the prior task's answer or solution. Raw learner responses, private expected-answer facts, assessment output, evaluator reasoning, and deficit claims derived from Unconfirmed performance remain private on every route.
